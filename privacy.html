Densha Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Densha – Focus Timer (“Densha”, “the app”) is made by Robert Maeda. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: Densha doesn't collect your data. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your rides, focus time, tags, stamps, coins, Rail Miles, shop items, streak and settings are stored on your iPhone, in the app's own storage and in a shared container its widgets and Live Activity can read. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. If you're signed in to iCloud, Densha also backs this up to your own iCloud account (you can turn that off in Settings → Backup), so a new iPhone or a reinstall can restore it. The backup is stored by Apple in your iCloud; we can't see it. Without the backup, deleting the app deletes this data.

Screen Time

Densha uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls) to hold the apps you choose during a ride. Apple gives the app opaque tokens for your selection, not the names of the apps or websites, and no information about how you use them. The selection never leaves your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions, the lifetime pass and coin packs are sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Densha never sees your payment details. The app checks your purchases with Apple's StoreKit on the device to unlock what you bought.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No reminders or marketing notifications. The only notification is the optional arrival alert, which is off unless you turn it on in Settings; it's scheduled on your iPhone and sends nothing anywhere.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location access. Your home city is something you pick, and it stays on the device.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information, because we don't have any.

Apple's services

The map uses Apple Maps, and purchases use the App Store. Those services are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Densha doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
