The Densha Pass

Densha requires a Densha Pass: a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

  • Free trial. The yearly plan includes a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it converts to a paid yearly subscription.
  • Automatic renewal. Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged within 24 hours before renewal.
  • Cancelling. Manage or cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the period ends.
  • Lifetime. A lifetime pass is a one-time purchase that doesn't renew.
