Densha – Focus Timer

Every focus session is a train ride across Japan. Pick two stations on the JR and Shinkansen network, put your distracting apps behind the ticket gate, and ride the real track until you arrive. Arrive to earn coins and a station stamp; get off early and you earn nothing.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: contact@robertmaeda.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

How do I restore my Densha Pass? Open Settings in the app (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription? In your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Densha.

Apps aren't being held during a ride. Densha needs Screen Time permission. Allow it when the app asks, or turn it on in iOS Settings → Screen Time.

Can I turn the announcements off? Yes: Settings in the app → Sound.
